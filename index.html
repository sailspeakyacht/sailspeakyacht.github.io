How a booking works

1. Pick a boat

Speedboats, fishing days, catamarans, motor yachts. Length, guest count and home port sit on the listing.

2. Talk to the captain

The page belongs to the person who will take you out. Ask about the route, the weather window, kids, fishing gear.

3. Pay into a hold

Funds are frozen on the site. The owner is paid after the rental period. A cancelled trip is replaced or refunded.

Why the rating exists

After the charter, guest and captain write about each other. That score is how the search list is ordered. A captain who cancels often, or who collects poor notes, drops. In a bad stretch the boats are taken off search altogether.

This page is a brand note for SailSpeak, not a second booking desk. Reservations stay on the official site.

For captains

Adding a boat takes a few minutes. You get a public page, messages from guests, and the same payment hold in your favour — an insurance deposit plus release after the rental. Traffic on the site is people already looking for a boat in your water.

Short FAQ

Is SailSpeak an agency?

No. Listings are the captains’. The site holds the money and the reviews.

What if the captain cancels?

You are offered another boat or the payment comes back.

Where do I actually book?

Only on sailspeak.com. This page is a description of the product, not a second checkout.
